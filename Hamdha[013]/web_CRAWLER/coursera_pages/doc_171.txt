Frequently Asked Questions What is the difference between the Teams and Coursera for Government plan? Coursera for Teams is the top solution for managing skill development objectives for smaller teams with 5 to 125 members. Coursera’s Government Plan is ideal for government organizations pursuing larger employee upskilling or workforce development programs. Both Teams and Coursera for Government offer unlimited access to 200,000 short videos, 10,600+ courses, 2,000+ Guided Projects and 1,400+ Specializations. The Coursera for Government plan offers additional access to LevelSets, SkillSets, Coursera Academies, and Coursera’s solutions design. What’s the difference between Coursera for Teams and Coursera Plus? Coursera Plus is a subscription for individuals seeking flexible learning. Coursera for Teams helps businesses train their employees with features like skill tracking and proficiency testing, as well as access to a library of almost 200,000 short videos and labs. → See the full Coursera for Teams content catalog → See the full Coursera Plus content catalog What are my payment options?
