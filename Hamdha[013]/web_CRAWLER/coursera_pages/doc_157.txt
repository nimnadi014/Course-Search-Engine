DATA HIGHLIGHT: PRODUCTIVITY GAINS Maximizing productivity with flexible learning Asynchronous digital learning allows employees to advance their skills at their own pace, leading to significant professional growth and increased productivity. The study revealed that this approach generated $918,000 in additional value from productivity gains , showcasing the powerful impact of tailored learning solutions. Get study DATA HIGHLIGHT: COST SAVINGS Reskilling leads to significant savings A composite organization representative of interviewed customers that use Coursera for employee skill development achieved substantial savings totaling $1.2M in two key areas. First, the vast course catalog reduces the need for external certifications, saving $795,000 over three years. Second, reskilling existing staff cuts talent acquisition costs, saving $407,000 in the same period.
