He currently serves as Chairman of the Coursera Board and is a leading voice in artificial intelligence (AI) and online education. Dr. Ng was the founding lead of the Google Brain deep learning project and later served as Chief Scientist at Baidu, where he led the company’s global AI strategy and infrastructure. He is also an adjunct professor at Stanford University, where he has taught in the computer science department for over two decades. In 2011, Dr. Ng led the development of Stanford’s first Massive Open Online Course (MOOC) platform and launched an online machine learning class that reached over 100,000 students—an initiative that inspired the creation of Coursera. He is the Managing General Partner of AI Fund, a venture studio supporting entrepreneurs in building AI companies, and leads DeepLearning.AI, an education technology company focused on AI training. In 2024, he joined Amazon’s Board of Directors. Dr. Ng has authored or co-authored over 200 research papers in machine learning, robotics, and related fields. In 2013, he was named to the Time 100 list of the world’s most influential people. He holds degrees from Carnegie Mellon University, MIT, and the University of California, Berkeley. At Amazon, where he spent 23 years, Greg served as Technical Advisor to Jeff Bezos and led the development of Amazon Alexa/Echo from inception to launch and beyond, establishing it as a pioneering AI product that has been distributed on over 100 million devices. He also oversaw Prime Video’s global expansion, leading its foray into live sports and implementing live streaming technology on the platform.
From 2020 to 2023, Greg was a key leader at Compass, the leading real estate brokerage in the US. He first served as Chief Product Officer and later as Chief Operating Officer, helping the company go public and leading the development of its industry-leading technology platform.
In December 2024, he joined the Bose Corporation Board. Greg holds a Bachelor of Arts from Williams College and is an inventor or co-inventor on 71 US patents. Read More Patrick Supanc Chief Product Officer Patrick Supanc joined Coursera as Chief Product Officer in June 2025, bringing over two decades of global leadership experience at the intersection of education, technology, and commerce. In this role, he leads Coursera’s global product strategy and development of innovative learning experiences to enable millions of learners and thousands of enterprise customers worldwide.
Prior to Coursera, Patrick was a senior product executive and general manager at Amazon, where he led initiatives including subscription growth and benefit engagement for Prime in the U.S., the global execution of Prime Day and Black Friday, and the expansion of Amazon Hub’s pickup and return network to a dozen countries, attracting millions of new customers. He also led Higher Education products for Kindle, enhancing digital learning experiences for college and adult learners.
Before Amazon, Patrick was a senior executive at Pearson, where he spearheaded adaptive learning solutions and launched the company’s first AI-powered learning app for college-aspiring students. He has also held leadership roles at Blackboard and served as an education specialist at the World Bank.
Patrick holds degrees in public policy and international affairs from Princeton and Harvard and was a Fulbright Scholar in Chile.
