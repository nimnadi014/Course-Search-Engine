critical process in data science that involves summarizing and visualizing datasets to uncover patterns, trends, and anomalies. It serves as a preliminary step before applying more complex statistical analyses or machine learning models. EDA is important because it helps data professionals understand the underlying structure of the data, identify potential issues, and formulate hypotheses. By engaging with the data visually and descriptively, analysts can make informed decisions about the next steps in their analysis, ensuring that the insights derived are both accurate and meaningful. ‎ Chevron Right What jobs can I get with Exploratory Data Analysis skills? ‎ A variety of job roles utilize exploratory data analysis skills.
