Mastering these skills can qualify you for roles in business analysis, data analysis, and other positions requiring data-informed decision making. What's the difference between Excel and Power BI, and why learn both in this specialization? Excel is excellent for data manipulation, basic analysis, and calculations, while Power BI excels at creating interactive visualizations, dashboards, and handling larger datasets. This specialization teaches both because they complement each other in a business environment—many analysts use Excel for data preparation and Power BI for visualization and sharing insights.
