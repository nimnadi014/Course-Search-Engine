It should take you between three and five months to complete this Specialization. You will be assigned approximately one to three hours worth of work to complete every week for the seventeen weeks it takes to complete the three courses. What background knowledge is necessary? To be successful in this Specialization you should have an understanding of the basics of using a Windows-based computer. Experience with printing and using a web browser are an asset, but not required. Do I need to take the courses in a specific order? No. We recommend taking the Work Smarter with Microsoft  Word course first as this contains a module that presents an overview of Microsoft 365 and the features available with different subscriptions. However, there is no need to take the courses in the order they are presented. The content in the courses don't build on information from earlier courses. Will I earn university credit for completing the Specialization? In short, no. Completing this Specialization will not earn you professional or academic credits.
