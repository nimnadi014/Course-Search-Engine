Career opportunities include management, consulting, analytics, entrepreneurship, and other high-demand jobs. Can I try a course before I apply or enroll in the full degree? Yes, you can start with Specializations and courses on Coursera to try out the asynchronous portions of iMSM coursework before or while you apply. What appears on the degree certificate? Upon completion of the iMSM program, students receive a Master of Science in Management degree from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign – the same degree conferred to on-campus students in the MSM program.
