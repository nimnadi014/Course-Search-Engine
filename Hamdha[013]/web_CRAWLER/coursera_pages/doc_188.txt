The Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence (MS-AI) is designed for learners who want to specialize deeply in AI and machine learning, positioning themselves at the forefront of technological innovation. This degree is a focused, deep dive into the most powerful technologies reshaping our world. You will gain advanced expertise in neural networks, deep learning, generative AI, and reinforcement learning. The curriculum is highly applied, preparing you to design, train, and deploy the intelligent systems that power modern products and services. You’ll acquire the end-to-end skills to manage projects through the entire AI lifecycle, from model building to scalable deployment. In addition to technical mastery, you will explore the critical context of responsible AI, learning to mitigate bias and understand the societal impact of your work. Program admission is performance-based, determined by your success in three preliminary courses, not your academic history, making a career in the high-growth field of artificial intelligence accessible. You’ll graduate from a top-ranked global research university, prepared for essential roles like Machine Learning Engineer, NLP Engineer, or Applied Scientist in high-growth, AI-driven industries. June 13, 2025 Frequently asked questions What appears on the diploma? Is it the same degree as the on-campus program? Yes, it’s the same as the on-campus program. Upon program completion, you’ll receive your diploma. It’s a degree conferred for a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence, and your diploma won’t mention Coursera or online study. Is the program accredited? Yes, the MS-AI on Coursera is included in the University of Colorado Boulder’s overall accreditation by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC). Can I try a course before I enroll in the graded pathway course? Yes, you can enroll in the non-credit experience. This allows you to complete some coursework in MS-AI on Coursera courses before committing to the degree program. You can upgrade to for-credit at any time during your learning journey. You’ll need to complete additional graded assignments to earn CU credit (this material is only available after you pay tuition). How many courses can students take at a time? This degree is self-paced, with no minimum or maximum number of courses required per session. We recommend you take just one course in the first session as you adjust to the demands of this graduate-level program. After that, if you’re ready to take on a full-time graduate-level course load,  we recommend three courses - or fewer - per session. Taking three courses per session, you can expect to complete the degree in about two years. To graduate, you must complete all courses (30 total credit hours) within eight years. It’s CU Boulder Graduate School policy that you can’t register for more than 15 credits in two sessions (one semester). If you’d like to enroll in more than 15 courses in two sessions, you’ll need to go through a petition process to demonstrate your ability to succeed with the additional course load. Will there be a graduation ceremony? Yes. When you graduate from the program, you’re welcome to attend the on-campus graduation ceremonies (if you choose). Can I switch between online and on-campus programs? No. The MS-AI on Coursera program and on-campus programs are not interchangeable. If you start the Coursera degree, you can’t switch to the on-campus program. Do the same faculty teach online and on-campus courses? Yes, both versions of the program are overseen by the same highly experienced faculty. Are there online office hours with instructors? Dedicated course facilitators hold weekly office hours where you can interact and ask questions about the material being covered. Do online students have access to campus facilities? As an MS-AI on Coursera student, you don’t have access to campus facilities, but you can access a wide range of online resources. This includes CU Boulder libraries, career services and alumni services. Will online students get a student ID card? Students taking for-credit classes receive an IdentiKey, which includes your CU Boulder login name and password. Your IdentiKey uniquely identifies you and acts as your student identification number. Students can also receive a physical Buff OneCard (student ID card) for $30 by emailing a headshot, photo ID, and mailing address to boc@colorado.edu from your colorado.edu student email address. Are internships part of the program? Not at this time. Can I transfer credits into the program? From CU programs on Coursera: Yes - you can use up to six graduate-level credit hours of courses from other CU programs on Coursera as elective credits toward the MS-AI degree on Coursera. All courses must be graduate level, offered on Coursera, and meet all applicable academic standards. This includes all courses offered by the ME-EM , MS-DS , MS-CS and MS-EE programs on Coursera. Credit from these courses cannot be applied toward MS-CS requirements: DTSA 5302 Cybersecurity for Data Science DTSA 5303 Ethical Issues in Data Science DTSA 5501 Algorithms for Searching, Sorting, and Indexing DTSA 5502 Trees and Graphs: Basics DTSA 5707 Deep Learning Applications for Computer Vision Courses can’t be counted twice toward two credentials of the same level. This means: you can apply credit from a particular course toward one graduate certificate and one graduate degree you can’t apply credit from a particular course toward two graduate certificates or two graduate degrees. CU certificates on Coursera are automatically awarded once all requirements are met. From other CU programs: No, this program doesn’t currently accept transfer credit from CU programs (other than those listed above). From other institutions: No, this program doesn’t currently accept transfer credit from other institution. When do I need to pay? Can I pay my tuition fees course by course? Or by semester? With pay-as-you-go tuition, you only need to pay for the courses you’re taking in the upcoming session when you enroll. Sessions are eight weeks long, and there are six enrollment sessions per year. Tuition is $525 per credit hour and $15,750 for the full 30-credit master’s degree. What coursework or skills training is recommended before enrolling in the pathway courses? The ideal preparation would include: A strong foundation in computer science (via academic or professional background) Working knowledge of at least one programming language Advanced knowledge in linear algebra, discrete math, probability and statistics (calculus required for select electives – see individual course descriptions for details). What are the English language proficiency requirements for enrollment  in the pathway courses? There are no English proficiency tests required for admission. The process is entirely based on your performance on the courses. To be admitted onto CU Boulder's MS-AI, you must complete a three-course pathway for CU credit with a B or better in each course. Are there any additional requirements for international students? The MS-AI on Coursera is open to students around the world. However, due to current restrictions imposed by the U.S. Department of Treasury’s Office of Foreign Assets Control, we can’t provide online courses to the following countries: Cuba, Iran, Sudan, North Korea, Syria, and the Crimea Region of Ukraine. See CU Online Course Delivery Restrictions for more details. Can I earn both an Artificial Intelligence Graduate Certificate and the MS-AI? You can earn an Artificial Intelligence Graduate Certificate on your way to an MS-AI degree without needing more than 30 credits. This is possible because multiple courses are shared between the MS-AI and AI Graduate Certificate. That means you can apply credits from one course toward both your graduate degree and your graduate certificate. To earn a CU graduate certificate on Coursera, you must declare your certificate via your student Buff Portal and maintain a cumulative certificate GPA of 3.00 or higher. Individual certificates may have additional requirements. Make sure you complete all steps to earn the graduate certificate(s) you are most interested in. Additional steps are required to earn graduate certificates offered by CU Boulder degree programs on Coursera other than your own program. Note that the Artificial Intelligence Graduate Certificate is part of CU Boulder's MS in Computer Science (MS-CS) program. Your program’s student handbook outlines those steps and other important considerations, including rules preventing students from double counting courses between multiple graduate certificates. How do I upgrade and earn credit from CU Boulder? You can upgrade and pay tuition during any open enrollment period to earn graduate-level CU Boulder credit for your master’s degree. See How It Works .
