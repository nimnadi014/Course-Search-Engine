As organizations worldwide increasingly transfer to digital systems, many organizations need trained cybersecurity professionals to help ensure the security of their information and systems. The cybersecurity skills you learn in this program will qualify you for an entry-level role as a Cybersecurity Analyst. How long does it take to complete the Professional Certificate? It typically takes between 4 to 8 months to complete the 8 courses. But some learners may go through the content faster. What background knowledge is necessary? No previous cybersecurity knowledge or experience is required, but you should be comfortable working with computers, be willing to develop new technical skills, enjoy collaborative problem solving, and communicating solutions. Does this program help me prepare for the CompTIA Security+ certification exam? Yes, this training program can help prepare you for the CompTIA Security+ certification exam administered by CompTIA. The self-paced courses in this program are intended to equip you with the knowledge necessary to kick start your career in cybersecurity and help you prepare for the CompTIA Security+ certification exam.
