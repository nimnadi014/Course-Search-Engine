EU AI Act Playbook: A Guide to AI Literacy and Compliance | Coursera For Individuals For Businesses For Universities For Governments Why Coursera Global Reports Resources Contact sales E-book Navigating the EU AI Act: A Playbook for Building an AI-Literate Workforce Ensure your organisation is compliant with Article 4 of the EU AI Act Are you ready for the EU AI Act? What’s inside The EU AI Act—one of the world’s first comprehensive AI laws—requires public and private organisations to ensure employees have the right level of AI literacy based on their roles and backgrounds. This playbook offers clear, actionable guidance to help leaders in business, government, and workforce development meet compliance and build future-ready teams. Who should read this: HR & L&D professionals Deliver scalable, role-specific AI training to meet new regulations. Public sector and workforce leaders Align AI skills with national strategies to promote inclusion and innovation. Tech and digital transformation leaders Ensure teams—from compliance to engineering—use AI safely and ethically. You’ll learn how to: navigate EU AI Act essentials, including what Article 4 requires, who’s impacted, and what compliance looks like implement AI literacy best practices, with real-world examples from Microsoft, Orange, and Schneider Electric activate training with skills assessments, role-based AI courses, and documentation templates AI literacy isn’t just a compliance checkbox—it's a fundamental pillar of our overall AI governance framework, connected directly to our strategic priorities and validated at the highest levels of leadership.
