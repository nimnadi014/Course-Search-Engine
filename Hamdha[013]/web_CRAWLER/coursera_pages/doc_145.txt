Chief Digital Officer, PwC India PWC Frequently asked questions What’s the difference between the Team and Enterprise plans? Coursera for Teams is for groups of 5 to 125 people and offers our full catalog of courses, customization tools like learning paths, translations and an interactive AI guide for learners, and tracking features. Designed for organizations with over 125 users, our Enterprise plan offers even more. It includes AI-assisted custom course building, dedicated customer success managers (with a minimum annual contract), and enterprise-level integrations for an extra fee. What’s the difference between Coursera for Teams and Coursera Plus? Coursera Plus is a subscription for individuals seeking flexible learning. Coursera for Teams helps businesses train their employees with features like skill tracking and proficiency testing, as well as access to a library of almost 200,000 short videos and labs. → See the full Coursera for Teams content catalog → See the full Coursera Plus content catalog Can my employees learn in languages other than English? Coursera’s Team plan for 5 to 125 users is priced at $319 per user annually. Pricing for Enterprise plans for 125+ users requires consultation with one of our advisors. Many customers consider the Enterprise plan as it features personalized support from our solution consulting team and a dedicated customer success manager (annual contract value threshold must be met). Coursera can also help with integrations and configurations—such as SSO and LMS—for customers who work with other vendors. Does Coursera partner with other LMS/LXP providers?
