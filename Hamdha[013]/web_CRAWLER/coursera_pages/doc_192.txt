What is a PGDM and how is it different from an MBA? The full form of PGDM is Post Graduate Diploma in Management. These two-year programs teach students the technical and workplace skills required to successfully pursue a career in management across different industries. The PGDM full form is an alternative on a similar level to an MBA. This diploma program typically takes around four semesters (or six trimesters). The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) approves the PGDM institutions. The main difference between a PGDM and an MBA is the institution that offers the programs. An MBA, or Master in Business Administration, is a degree that universities confer, and only universities can give degrees. A PGDM is a diploma offered by autonomous institutions approved by the AICTE. A PGDM is equivalent to an MBA, and recruiters may look for either when considering hiring candidates.
