What background knowledge is necessary? None. The first course in this professional certificate provides foundational cloud knowledge and an introduction to a broad range of AWS Services. What is a Solution Architect? Solutions Architects (SAs, as they are known at AWS) are responsible for managing an organization’s cloud computing architecture. They have in-depth knowledge of the architectural principles and services used to develop technical cloud strategy, assist with cloud migration efforts, review workload architectures, and provide guidance on how to address high-risk issues. What does a Solution Architect do? A Solutions Architect is knowledgeable of the systems and processes to guide the broad technical design which outlines a solution’s architecture components. The solution architect offers a continual holistic view into how systems are or need to be integrated, what data silos are present and recommends technology best practices throughout the project’s lifecycle.
