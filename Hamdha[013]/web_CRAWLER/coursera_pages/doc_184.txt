Frequently asked questions What appears on the degree certificate? Is it the same degree as the on-campus programme? This course has been developed specifically for online delivery. Your degree certificate and transcript will be awarded from the University of Huddersfield, and won’t contain any references to Coursera or online/distance learning. Is the programme fully online, or are there any in-person requirements such as final exams? Yes, the programme is fully online, and there are no in-person requirements. All assessments will take place online and the programme is expected to be completed fully remotely. Are there online office hours with instructors? Academics will be available during online tutor hours and by appointment. Our Student Support team will be available between 9-5 Monday to Friday (UK time) by email. Who can I contact if I have any more questions? Our Distance Learning Unit Team is available to answer any questions you may have about our courses, your application, entry requirements, funding your studies, and any other issues you may have. Please email via dl-enquiry@hud.ac.uk for assistance. Are there networking opportunities to interact with fellow students? Yes - you’ll join a global community and benefit from peer-to-peer learning and networking with professionals from all over the world. How long does it take to complete the programme if I’m working while studying? You can complete the programme in six years, studying part time. Will there be a graduation ceremony? When you complete the programme, you’ll have the opportunity to visit Huddersfield in person to attend your graduation ceremony. Here, you’ll be able to celebrate your success and meet your coursemates and tutors in person. Do the same faculty teach online and on-campus courses? Yes, all our distance learning courses are delivered by academics from the University of Huddersfield, meaning you’ll benefit from the same level of teaching excellence as our on-campus students.
