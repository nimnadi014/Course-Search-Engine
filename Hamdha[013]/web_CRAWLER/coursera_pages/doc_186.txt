Check out the latest updates from Illinois Tech Earning an M.B.A. While Hiking the Pacific Crest Trail: Meet Illinois Tech Student Janie Butler Janie Butler's passion for green chemistry began during her undergraduate studies and has guided her career in nonprofit education, focusing on sustainable STEM initiatives. To further her impact, she pursued an online MBA at Illinois Tech, known for its tech-focused curriculum and flexible schedule. This flexibility allowed Butler to also fulfill her dream of hiking the 2,650-mile Pacific Crest Trail. Butler chose the MBA to enhance her leadership and business skills, valuing its comprehensive approach over shorter certifications. The program’s focus on innovation, its adaptable format, and the opportunity to engage deeply with subjects like leadership and marketing strategy appealed to her. Balancing her studies with hiking, she found motivation in the MBA’s structure and her passion for learning. Experiencing wildfires during her hike reinforced her commitment to using her education to combat climate change and support nonprofit missions, aiming to empower future scientists and environmental advocates. Check it out! Frequently asked questions Is the program accredited? Yes, it is accredited by the AACSB (Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business), which is the world’s leading accreditation body for business schools. Can I try a course before I enroll in the graded pathway course? Yes, learners can enroll in stackable open content to experience the content in the pathway courses. Find a course here ! How many courses can students take at a time? Up to 12 credits a semester. Will there be a graduation ceremony? Illinois Tech holds its Commencement ceremony once per year in May, and all students will have the option to attend. Do the same faculty teach online and on-campus courses? Yes, you’ll learn from the same outstanding faculty while enjoying the same emphasis on faculty-student interaction, rigorous academics, and hands-on learning as your on-campus peers. Are there online office hours with instructors? Yes, there will be online office hours. Do online students have access to on-campus facilities? Yes, Illinois Tech’s online M.B.A. students are welcome to visit our commuter-friendly campuses—the historic Mies Campus in Bronzeville or the downtown Chicago campus within Conviser Law Center, which is a block away from Union Station—at any time to meet faculty or staff in person, tour facilities, use the library, or attend events. Will online students get a student ID card? Illinois Tech’s online M.B.A. students are welcome to submit a photo that meets the stated requirements and receive a HawkCard (Illinois Tech Student ID). After the photo has been approved and the HawkCard produced, you can arrange to have the card mailed to you by emailing the Campus Business Services Center; the process is not automatic. Can students transfer between the online and in-person version of this program? In general, transfer between the in-person MBA program and the online Coursera MBA program is not allowed; if you would like to appeal for further consideration, please email MBA-support@iit.edu. Can I register for the Illinois Tech Coursera program with my VA education and training benefits? First-Time Applicants (New Students) If you have not processed VA benefits at Illinois Tech before, please complete the following steps: Apply for VA benefits with the United States Department of Veterans Affairs (if you have already not done so) and obtain your certificate of eligibility. For more information on how to apply, please visit the Department of Veterans Affairs website . Send a digital copy of your certificate of eligibility via email to Christopher Lee at clee106@iit.edu. Register for PBA courses via the Application and PBA Course Registration Form. This is important to avoid tuition charges at registration time. Certifying VA benefits can take up to three weeks after the start of the term. You are still responsible for the tuition if the VA benefit certification does not go through for any reason.
