What is the Deep Learning Specialization on Coursera? The Deep Learning Specialization is a five-course series taught by Andrew Ng that covers the foundations of deep learning, neural networks , CNNs , sequence models, and practical implementation with TensorFlow and Python . It's ideal for learners with some programming and math experience who want to work in AI . Are DeepLearning.AI certificates recognized by employers? Yes. What is the difference between a Specialization and a Professional Certificate from DeepLearning.AI? A Specialization is a series of related courses that focus on a specific topic, like deep learning or NLP . A Professional Certificate is designed to prepare you for a career path and may include hands-on projects, assessments, and job-aligned skills in areas like generative AI or machine learning engineering.
