Learn more about social media jobs Opens in a new tab . What is a Meta certification? A Meta Certification is an industry credential that signifies proficiency on Meta technologies such as Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and WhatsApp. Meta offers several certifications across digital marketing, marketing analytics and software engineering. As part of the Meta Social Media Marketing Professional Certificate, you’ll take an online proctored exam for the Meta Digital Marketing Associate Certification to become a Meta Certified Professional. This exam is currently available in English, Portuguese and Spanish. What can I do with a Meta certification? Through this Professional Certificate program, you’ll learn how to develop a social media presence for a business, including creating engaging content, interacting with audiences, managing a content calendar, and evaluating the success of your posts. You’ll also learn how to create effective ads for social media and how to create ads in Meta Ads Manager. Earning your Meta Social Media Marketing Professional Certificate signals to hiring managers and your professional peers, a level of proficiency in these marketing skills. You can further demonstrate your expertise by earning your Meta Digital Marketing Associate Certification upon completion. Both of these credentials help you to establish industry credibility and prove your expertise to potential employers or clients. Is the Meta certification free? As part of the Meta Social Media Marketing Professional Certificate program on Coursera, you will be able to take the Meta Digital Marketing Associate Certification exam for free. If you do not pass the exam the first time, you will be eligible to take the exam one additional time free of charge. Any subsequent retakes will take place with PearsonVUE via OnVUE or an in-person center, and would result in an exam fee which varies depending on your location. What are the technical requirements for me to take my Meta Digital Marketing Associate Certification exam? In course 6, the Capstone, you will sign up to take the Meta Digital Marketing Associate Certification exam, free of charge. If you do not pass the exam the first time, you will be eligible to take the exam one additional time at no cost. Any subsequent retakes will take place with PearsonVUE via OnVUE or an in-person center, and would result in an exam fee which varies depending on your location. The exam is online and proctored and available in English, Portuguese and Spanish. You need to have conversational level communication skills in these languages in order to communicate with the proctor. To take the exam, you will need access to a laptop or desktop computer, using a Mac or Windows operating system, with a webcam for 105 Mins, uninterrupted. In addition, you will need a strong internet connection and be able to download the required applications (Chrome and Honorlock) before the exam starts. You will receive email communications that guide you through the requirements. Please make sure you read the instructions carefully before the exam. Will I only learn about Facebook and Instagram? No. You’ll gain a basic understanding of how the main social media platforms — like Twitter, Pinterest, LinkedIn, TikTok, and more — function and how businesses can use them for their marketing needs. You will learn how to develop effective ads for different social media platforms, but this program specifically focuses on how to develop these ads using Meta Ads Manager (for advertising on both Facebook and Instagram). While ad creation tools for various social media platforms differ, understanding how Meta Ads Manager works will help you quickly find your way around other advertising tools (though this program does not focus on how to use other advertising tools).
