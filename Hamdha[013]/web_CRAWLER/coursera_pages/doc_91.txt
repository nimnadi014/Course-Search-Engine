Whether you’re completely new to the job field or have had some exposure, a Payroll Specialist Professional Certificate is the right program for you. What is a Payroll Specialist? A Payroll Specialist (also known as Payroll Administrator / Payroll Coordinator / Payroll Officer / Payroll Professional) is responsible for ensuring accurate and timely processing of employee salaries and wages within an organization. What does a Payroll Specialist do? Payroll Specialists handle tasks such as calculating hours worked, deductions, and tax withholdings, and ensuring compliance with federal-level payroll regulations. They also address employee inquiries regarding pay and resolve any payroll-related issues that may arise. Do I need to take the courses in a specific order?
