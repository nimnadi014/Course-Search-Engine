Frequently asked questions Can I choose a computer science specialism to study? Yes - the programme includes a professional certification course in the specialism of your choice, allowing you to earn a separate industry certificate delivered by a leading technology company such as Google, AWS or IBM. What career options will I have with this degree? You will develop the critical skills needed for in-demand jobs in computer science and programming, data, web and mobile development, cloud architecture, artificial intelligence, software engineering and more. What will appear on my degree certificate? Is it the same degree as the on-campus programme? The degree certificate you receive when you complete the programme will be awarded for a Master of Science in Computer Science from Heriot-Watt University. It doesn’t mention ‘online’ or ‘Coursera’ anywhere on the degree certificate. Who can I contact if I have questions about the admissions process? If you have any questions about the admissions process or the programme, please do not hesitate to get in touch via hwoadmissions@hw.ac.uk. Does the programme have any English language requirements? Yes - the programme is taught in English, so evidence of proficiency in English is a requirement. Will I have to pay for my exams or study materials? No - the tuition fees include all exams, known as Final Online Assessments, study materials and other expenses associated with the course. Will there be a graduation ceremony? As a Heriot-Watt student, you’ll be welcome to attend and receive your degree certificate alongside the on-campus students in Edinburgh, Dubai or Malaysia when you complete the programme. Attendance at the graduation ceremony is free for graduates. Tickets for guests and tickets to the graduation reception will be charged at an additional cost. Can I pause my studies? Yes  – because of the modular, flexible nature of the programme you can register for courses when it suits you, allowing you to progress at your own pace and pause your studies when you need to. However, if you need to register a formal interruption of studies for any reason, you can do so without penalty or loss of progress for up to 12 months. How does the online format work? Our teaching methods will be diverse, engaging, and interactive. You'll experience a blend of asynchronous learning, engaging digital materials and multimedia, hands-on activities, and job-aligned projects. The programme is designed around real-life examples, enabling you to develop your skills thoroughly before assessment. Do the same faculty teach online and on-campus courses? Yes, all of these courses are designed and delivered by academics from Heriot-Watt University, meaning you’ll benefit from the same level of academic excellence as our on-campus students. What is the time commitment required? How flexible is the schedule? This programme is purposefully designed with flexibility to accommodate working professionals. The time commitment can differ for each student based on their individual responsibilities and study habits. However, as a general recommendation, we suggest dedicating 16-18 hours per week to your studies to successfully complete the programme. Show all 11 frequently asked questions More questions?
