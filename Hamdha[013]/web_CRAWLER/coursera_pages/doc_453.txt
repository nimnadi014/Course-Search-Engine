They consist of two neural networks, the generator and the discriminator, which work against each other in a game-like scenario. The generator creates new data instances, while the discriminator evaluates them against real data, providing feedback to improve the generator's output. This innovative approach is crucial in various fields, including art, music, and even medicine, as it enables the creation of realistic images, videos, and other media.
