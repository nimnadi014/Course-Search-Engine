Last updated on August 20, 2025 Article Show more Frequently asked questions Are these degrees recognised? Degrees from Indian universities on Coursera are from leading institutions and hold the same academic value as other recognized degrees from those universities. Can I apply for government jobs with these degrees? Yes, for most roles that require a recognized degree, these programs meet the eligibility criteria. However, some specific government jobs may have additional requirements, so it’s always best to check the particular job notification. Do employers respect online degrees? Employers increasingly recognize online degrees from reputed universities, especially when they focus on practical, job-relevant skills. With these degrees, you’re learning from top Indian institutions known for academic excellence. Which degree is right for me? It depends on your career goals, educational background, and interests. Whether you’re looking to break into tech, business, or data science, Coursera offers degrees from leading Indian universities. What if I find the coursework difficult? You’ll have access to faculty office hours, support teams, peer learning communities, and structured academic curriculum to help guide you. Are the exams and assessments fully online? Yes, these are fully online degrees, including assessments. Some programs may offer optional campus immersions, but they are not required. Can I study while working full-time? Absolutely! These degrees are built for working professionals and students balancing other commitments, with flexible schedules and modular learning. How are these degrees different from Coursera certificates? Degrees lead to a formal qualification (Bachelor’s or Master’s) from a university, while certificates focus on specific skills and are typically much shorter. A degree offers in-depth learning, university faculty engagement, and a recognized credential. Who will I study with? Your classmates will include learners from across India and globally, with most classes having students from 15+ nationalities -  giving you a diverse peer network and a global learning environment. Is there someone who can help me decide? Yes! Coursera has a dedicated team of Enrollment Representatives to guide you in choosing the right degree based on your career goals and background. You can also attend webinars featuring faculty, students, and alumni to get a clearer picture of the learning experience and career outcomes.
