The GenAI Skills Track is a structured AI training program that helps organizations upskill employees in generative AI. Created in collaboration with industry leaders such as DeepLearning.AI, Microsoft, and NVIDIA, it equips teams with essential GenAI skills to boost productivity, drive innovation, and ensure responsible AI use across business functions. How does the GenAI Skills Track help organizations upskill in AI? Coursera’s GenAI training provides employees with hands-on learning experiences in AI tools, large language models (LLMs), and automation. Through guided projects, real-world examples, and verified credentials, companies can upskill AI capabilities across departments while encouraging ethical and secure adoption practices. What topics are covered in the Generative AI Skills Track? The Generative AI Skills Track delivers comprehensive AI workforce training across key areas such as prompt engineering, AI ethics, automation, and data-driven decision-making. Employees learn how to apply generative AI skills effectively while building confidence in using GenAI tools for daily workflows. Who is the GenAI Skills Track designed for? This AI employee training program is ideal for professionals at every level, including analysts, marketers, developers, and executives. Coursera’s GenAI learning paths make it easy to tailor training to each role and skill level so every team member can develop the AI skills needed to contribute to business success. How does the GenAI Skills Track improve business outcomes? Organizations that invest in AI upskilling can increase efficiency, reduce repetitive work, and encourage innovation. Coursera’s AI training for employees helps teams automate processes, improve creativity, and make smarter decisions, resulting in measurable productivity gains and stronger business performance.
