This program is designed for individuals seeking to enter the fitness industry through 1-on-1 coaching and/or training. This is the perfect program for individuals with a passion and/or background in fitness. What does a Personal Fitness Trainer do? A personal fitness trainer works with clients and provides guidance and coaching for clients looking to improve their lives through fitness. The Personal Fitness Trainer assesses the fitness of clients and develops programs to help them achieve their goals. The Personal Fitness Trainer will also provide coaching during training sessions to ensure proper technique is used when exercising. The most important aspect of the Personal Fitness Trainer role is helping coach clients to change their behaviors to maintain long-term change of their habits. What background knowledge is necessary? There is no background knowledge necessary or any prerequisites for this program. How long does it take to complete this program? It takes between 3 to 5 months depending on your motivation to get through the program. Do I need to take the courses in a specific order? The only requirement is that the ‘Preparation for your Personal Fitness Trainer Exam’ course is completed last. This is the final course and includes your capstone hands-on projects, as well as your final exam preparation. The other 4 courses can be taken in any order. Where can Personal Fitness Trainers find work? These professionals have a variety of opportunities for work. Oftentimes they will gain employment in gyms, recreation centers, and other fitness facilities. Personal Fitness Trainers also have a unique opportunity to work independently in their own homes, client’s homes, or even through digital means with online coaching. What prerequisites are there for taking the Personal Fitness Trainer Exam? Prior to taking the Personal Fitness Trainer final exam, learners must hold a current CPR/AED certification, which can be completed in the ‘Preparation for your Personal Fitness Trainer Exam’ course if needed.
