Submit C4C Gen AI FAQs What is generative AI in higher education? Generative AI in higher education refers to the use of AI tools, applications, and frameworks to enhance learning, teaching, student services, and institutional operations. Universities adopt GenAI to build AI literacy for students, modernize curriculum, and improve efficiency across campus. How does Coursera help universities build AI literacy for students? Coursera offers industry-designed courses, hands-on projects, and role-specific learning paths that teach students how to use GenAI responsibly and effectively. These resources help institutions embed AI literacy for students directly into academic programs and co-curricular learning. Why is GenAI important for faculty and staff? Generative AI enables faculty to modernize instruction, automate routine tasks, improve grading and feedback, and enhance student engagement. For staff, GenAI supports workflow optimization and improved productivity across administrative units. How is Coursera’s GenAI offering different from consumer AI learning content? Coursera for Campus provides institution-wide solutions designed specifically for higher education. This includes academic integrity tools, LMS integration, teaching support, faculty enablement, and enterprise-level governance—capabilities not included in consumer GenAI products. Can generative AI content be integrated into existing university programs? Yes. Institutions can embed Coursera’s GenAI courses, certificates, guided projects, and teaching tools into current degree programs, general education, advising initiatives, and professional development pathways. This allows schools to scale gen ai in higher education without creating new curricula from scratch.
