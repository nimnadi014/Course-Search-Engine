You'll gain a foundational understanding of Python programming, Jupyter Notebooks, NumPy for numerical computing, and pandas for data manipulation and analysis—core tools used by professional data analysts. Do I need to take the course in a certain order? We highly recommend completing the courses in the order presented because the content in each course builds on information covered in earlier lessons. What is data science? Organizations of all types and sizes have business processes that generate massive volumes of data. Every moment, all sorts of information gets created by computers, the internet, phones, texts, streaming video, photographs, sensors, and much more. In the global digital landscape, data is increasingly imprecise, chaotic, and unstructured. As the speed and variety of data increases exponentially, organizations are struggling to keep pace. Data science is part of a field of study that uses raw data to create new ways of modeling and understanding the unknown. To gain insights, businesses rely on people with data science knowledge to acquire, organize, and interpret data, which helps inform internal projects and processes.
