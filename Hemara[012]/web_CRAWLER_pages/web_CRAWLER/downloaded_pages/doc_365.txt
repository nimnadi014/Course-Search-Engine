The CompTIA A+ certification (220-1201 & 220-1202) is the industry standard for launching a successful career in IT. It verifies that you have the foundational skills needed to support and troubleshoot hardware, software, networks, and operating systems across various environments. This 12-hour complete course is your one-stop solution for mastering both Core 1 (220-1201) and Core 2 (220-1202) of the updated CompTIA A+ exam series. Designed with beginners in mind, this course uses step-by-step video lectures, practical demos, hands-on labs, downloadable resources, and full-length practice exams to get you exam-ready and job-ready. Whether you’re starting from scratch or aiming to validate your skills, this course provides all the tools and guidance you need to pass your exams with confidence—and step into your first IT support role with credibility. Master cybersecurity basics , including malware types, secure protocols, and organizational security. Develop your knowledge of IT operational procedures , including documentation, scripting, change management, and professionalism.
