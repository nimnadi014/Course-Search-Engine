Canva is an online graphic design and publishing software that anyone can use—from non-graphic designers to professionals. The program launched in 2013 and now has millions of users. The tool is loaded with thousands of templates and easy-to-use features (such as photo effects, photos, videos, icons, shapes, stickers, animation effects, and a font library). You can make plenty of types of content, such as ads, invitations, business cards, documents, presentations, and marketing materials. Plus, the program lets you plan when to schedule and publish content on social media and elsewhere with a content planner. What careers use Canva? Though professional graphic designers can use Canva, the tool is also attractive to non-graphic designers. Small business owners and entrepreneurs, for example, are ideal users of Canva. Let’s say you own your own business and don’t want to spend a lot of money on professional graphic designing. In that case, Canva is a convenient tool that’ll allow you to create impressive graphics. Also, other non-graphic designer professionals may use Canva to support their content, such as copywriters, content marketers, bloggers, and social media marketing managers. What are some common Canva alternatives? Other similar tools to Canva are available. One, Crello, is considered to be a close alternative to Canva. This tool is known for its animation suite. Snappa, another alternative, provides various templates to help you design graphics for social media and blogs. Easil, a drag and drop editor, offers its users exclusive stock images that can’t be found elsewhere on the internet. Stencil has fewer templates to choose from than other Canva alternatives and is more limited; however, this easy-to-use tool lets non-graphic designers create good-looking graphics.
