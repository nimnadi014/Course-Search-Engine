Prove your ability to lead projects in any industry with this globally recognised certification. What is PMP Certification? The PMP recognises candidates skilled in managing the people, processes and business priorities of professional projects.PMI, the world's leading authority on project management, created the PMP to recognise project managers who have demonstrated their project leadership experience and expertise in any way of working. To earn the PMP certification, project managers must meet certain requirements and then pass a 180-question exam.The PMP exam is designed by project managers for project managers, so each question is relevant to real-world project management experience. The PMP exam is available in the following languages: Arabic, Chinese (Simplified + Traditional), English, French, German, Hebrew, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish (Poland), Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish (Mexico + Spain), and Turkish. PMP certification demonstrates that you are highly skilled in the following areas: Motivating employees and teams at all stages of project success. Use predictive, agile, and hybrid methods to determine which way of working is best for each project. Emphasise the success of projects and their impact on the overall strategic goals of the organisation. Who this course is for: Students who are about to take the PMP exam or those who did not do well in the previous exam and need to retake it.
